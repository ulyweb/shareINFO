# 🚀 AI Assistant — One-Click Setup Guide for macOS

Welcome! This package installs your own 100% private, voice-interactive AI assistant with persistent memory and 3D visualizers.

---

### 🛠️ Step 0: Quick Mac Prep (Run Once if you don't have Homebrew/Ollama)

Open your Mac **Terminal** (`Cmd + Space` -> type `Terminal`) and paste:

1. **Install Homebrew** (if you don't have it):
   /bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"

2. **Install Ollama** (Local AI Engine):
   brew install ollama
   (Or download the one-click Mac app from https://ollama.com)

---

### 📦 Step 1: Unzip the Project
In your Terminal:
unzip JarvisAI-macOS.zip -d ~/Documents/
cd ~/Documents/JarvisAI

---

### 🧙 Step 2: Run the Onboarding Wizard
./install_mac.sh

The installer will ask you:
1. "What is your name / what should the AI call you?" (e.g. Alex)
2. "What would you like to name your AI Assistant?" (e.g. Athena, Friday, AlexAI)
3. "Choose your AI Brain": Select [1] for 100% Offline Local Model (Ollama)
4. Select model: [1] qwen2.5:14b (Recommended for 16GB+ Macs) or [2] qwen2.5:7b (for 8GB Macs)

The installer will automatically download the model, build the environment, and warm the speech audio!

---

### 🎙️ Step 3: Launch Your Assistant!
./start_jarvis.sh

• Just speak out loud into your microphone (Hands-free listening is active).
• 3D Visualizer: Open http://127.0.0.1:8790 in your browser (Press Space for 3D flythrough).
• Memory: Any preferences or tasks you say ("Remember that...") are permanently saved!
